The 9th Web Audio Conference (10 years), co-organized by Ircam and Mozilla, was held from 19 to 21 November 2025 in Paris, France.

Tug F. O'Flaherty and Dr Anna Xambó presented a paper and performance related to the Sensing the Forest project:

 * O’Flaherty, T. F., Elmokadem, M. B., Xu, X., Manjunatha, K. N., Roma, G., Xenakis, G., Xambó Sedó, A., (2025) [“Sonification Mappings for Sensing Tree Stress: A DIY Approach”](https://zenodo.org/records/17642480). Proceedings of the Web Audio Conference 2025 (WAC 2025). Ircam/Mozilla, Paris, France.
 * Xambó, Anna (November 19, 2025). Sensing the Alice Holt Forest, live performance. Web Audio Conference 2025, IRCAM/Mozilla, Paris, France.

Part of this work relates to Tug's master final project [*Dendrostream: Exploring Tree Stress and Climate Change Through Sonification and Visualisation*](https://sensingtheforest.github.io/2025/12/23/dendrostream/) (MSc Sound and Music Computing) supervised by Anna Xambó. If you want to find out more about Tug's master project, you can find out more [here](https://sensingtheforest.github.io/2025/12/23/dendrostream/).
