Powered by S+T+ARTS, Sonar+D hosted the second edition of ‘AI Performance Playground’: a hacklab for 12 participants who were selected through our Open Call in February. These selected participants spent three days working with AI applied to engineering or music, under the direction of Anna Xambó and Ben Cantil, with the support of La Salle-URL’s IASLab. The audience was able to witness the fruits of their labour in a [public performance](https://web.archive.org/web/20250725160531/https://sonar.es/en/activity/ai-performance-playground-live) on Saturday at Sónar+D.
