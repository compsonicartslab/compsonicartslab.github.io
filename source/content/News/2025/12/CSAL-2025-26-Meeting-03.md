*Lab visit to "Peter Doig Exhibition: House of Music" at the Serpentine.*

**Day/time**: 11/12/2025 10.30am-12pm

**Location**: Serpentine, W2 3XA London

### Summary

Our third monthly meeting is held at the Serpentine to experience the [Peter Doig Exhibition: House of Music](https://www.serpentinegalleries.org/whats-on/peter-doig-house-of-music/) (thanks Merlin for the suggestion!) to celebrate the holiday season. The lab has already got the tickets for the group (free entrance, but you need to book).

> *Serpentine presents a new project by Peter Doig that explores the role of music, film, and sites of communal gathering, listening and creative exchange within his practice.*
 
The meeting point can be 10.15am-10.30am at a nice cafe next to the gallery.
