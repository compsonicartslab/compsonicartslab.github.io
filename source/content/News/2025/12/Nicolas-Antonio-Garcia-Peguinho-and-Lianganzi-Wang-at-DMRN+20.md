On Tuesday 16th December 2025, Nicolás António García-Peguinho and Lianganzi Wang presented their initial work related to their PhDs at the [Digital Music Research Network One-day Workshop 2025](https://www.qmul.ac.uk/dmrn/dmrn20/):

* [“Validating Few-shot Bird Vocalisation Detection through Causal Explanations”](https://sensingtheforest.github.io/assets/pdf/ReX_StF_DMRN_PosterAbstract.pdf), Nico García-Peguinho (Queen Mary University of London, UK), David Kelly, Melane Navaratnarajah, Hana Chockler (King’s College London, UK) and Anna Xambó Sedó (Queen Mary University of London, UK).
* [“Towards Real-Time, Stable Mapping from Multimodal Sensing to Interpretable Timbre Axes”](https://qmro.qmul.ac.uk/handle/123456789/120259), Lianganzi Wang, Iran R. Roman and Anna Xambó Sedó (Queen Mary University of London, UK).

You can find out more on the [DMRN+20 Proceedings](https://www.qmul.ac.uk/dmrn/media/dmrn/DMRN-20-Abstracts-15-Dec.pdf).
