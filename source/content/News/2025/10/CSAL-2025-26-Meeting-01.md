### Agenda

* Share your research in 5 minutes (your main research question)
* Share highlights of your workflows (how you manage reading, writing, coding, etc.)
* What's next for you

### Summary

Today we start a monthly group meeting so that you the lab members can connect and share their PhD and research work.

The meetings are designed to be hybrid, as some of the lab members are not based in London. 

For those attending onsite, we can go for lunch together afterwards (12pm-1pm). Feel free to bring your own lunch, and we can go to a hybrid place where you can also buy food if necessary.
