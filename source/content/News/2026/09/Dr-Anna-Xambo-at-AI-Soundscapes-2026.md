Anna was invited to give a lecture-workshop to explore creative AI applied to live coding and digital sampling at the [AI-Soundscapes Congress](https://zentrum-pop.de/ai-soundscapes/) organised by Zentrum Pop and hosted by Filmuniversität Babelsberg, Potsdam, Germany.

The talk, "Sampling, Live Coding, and AI: Improvising with Sound-based Data", presented ideas around sampling, live coding and creative AI. 

As part of the talk, Anna presented [MIRLCa on the web (MIRLCaweb)](https://mirlca-web.nb-nik.org) developed by Nikhil Dexter Bullock as part of his master's project supervised by Penny and Anna. This led to a vivid discussion on:

- What is a model?
- What is the intention when training the model?
- How do you want to use it?
- How training a binary classifier works?
- Are good/bad labels enough?
- When to stop training?

Thank you Chandra, Angela, Luise, Zentrum Pop and Film University Babelsberg for the invitation.
