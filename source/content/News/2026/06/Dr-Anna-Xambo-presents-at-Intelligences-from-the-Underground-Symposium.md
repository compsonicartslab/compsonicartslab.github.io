### Summary

Anna was invited to give a talk and a [performance](https://www.eventbrite.co.uk/e/intelligences-from-the-underground-tickets-1987501319950?utm-campaign=social&utm-content=attendeeshare&utm-medium=discovery&utm-term=listing&utm-source=cp&aff=ebdsshcopyurl) at the [Intelligences from the Underground Symposium](https://undergroundintelligences.com/symposium/), hosted at the Royal Conservatoire of Scotland, Glasgow, Scotland. This event was organised by Maria Sappho and Pierre Alexandre Tremblay. Thanks for the invitation!

> This talk presents my practice with live coding and sound sampling as a suitable approach for real-time creation and improvisation with personal and crowdsourced sounds. AI algorithms can aid in the process of searching, retrieving, and discovering new sounds and patterns, opening up possibilities for unexpected interactions between humans, machines, code, data, and the audience.
