### Summary

Anna was invited to co-facilitate the [AI Performance Playground 2026](https://web.archive.org/web/20260511122624/https://sonar.es/en/programme/sonar-d/open-call-hacklab-2026) (Sónar+D Hacklab) together with [Ben Cantil](https://encanti.com/) (DataMind Audio). It was a total blast! The quality of the groups was top-notch.

A group of 20 international artists were selected to explore the use of AI, driven by the questions: *What new possibilities for human-AI co-creativity do these tools offer? How might entangled collaborative practices of human-AI agencies challenge and expand this emerging field?*

Between 17th and 19th June, the artists explored, tinkered, hacked, experimented, invented and created collaborative technologies and performances that enquired into these questions. Ben wrote this blog post on [How to Make a Meal Out of Slop: Neuroaesthetics in Neural Audio](https://datamindaudio.ai/how-to-make-a-meal-out-of-slop-neuroaesthetics-in-neural-audio/) that summarises well the new aesthetics that emerge from these new technologies and interactions. The public-facing collaborative performance closed the final day of Sónar+D.

We thank Antònia Folguera, Ainhoa Fernandez, the [S+T+ARTS](https://starts.eu/) programme, the IASlab-La Salle and the entire Sonar+D team for the opportunity to explore new musical discourses and practices in such a spectacular setting, [Llotja de Mar](https://www.llotjademar.cat/es). Thank you.
