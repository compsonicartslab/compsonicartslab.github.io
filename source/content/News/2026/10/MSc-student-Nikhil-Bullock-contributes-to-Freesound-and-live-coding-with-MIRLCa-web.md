*A screenshot of the landing page of MIRLCa-web.*

As part of his thesis for the [MSc Sound and Music Computing](https://www.qmul.ac.uk/postgraduate/taught/coursefinder/courses/sound-and-music-computing-msc/) at Queen Mary University of London, [Nikhil Bullock](https://nb-nik.org/) has ported the SuperCollider extension [MIRLCa](https://github.com/axambo/MIRLCa), developed by Anna Xambó, to the browser. The project was supervised by the same Anna, together with [Panagiota Anastasopoulou](https://allholy.github.io/), a PhD student from the Music Technology Group, Universitat Pompeu Fabra, who’s part of the Freesound team, and who visited C4DM-CSAL in March-June 2026.

You can read more about Nikhil's project in this Freesound blog post: [MIRLCa-web: Live-coding with Freesound in the web browser](https://blog.freesound.org/?p=2444)

You can try the tool here: [https://mirlca-web.nb-nik.org/](https://mirlca-web.nb-nik.org/)

Congratulations, Nikhil!
