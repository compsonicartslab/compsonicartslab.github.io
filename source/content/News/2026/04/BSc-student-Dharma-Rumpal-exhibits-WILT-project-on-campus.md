*A closeup of the WILT project: Using Solar-Powered Kinetic Art to Communicate Climate Change.*

Dharma Rumpal, a graduate student from BSC FT Computer Science (EngC Pathway), Queen Mary University of London, decided to undertake a research project based on exploring climate awareness through solar-powered kinetic art under the supervision of Kok Ho Huen and Dr Anna Xambó. The project investigates air quality around the world combining scientific, engineering and artistic methods.

As part of her *WILT project: Using Solar-Powered Kinetic Art to Communicate Climate Change*, Dharma will be exhibiting the project as an installation on Thursday 9th April from 1–4pm and Friday 10th April from 2–5pm at rooms 101&102, the Hive, Queen Mary University of London Campus.

All welcome!

You can read more about Dharma's work in this blog post published at the Sensing the Forest blog: [WILT – Using Solar-Powered Kinetic Art to Communicate Climate Change](https://sensingtheforest.github.io/2026/10/01/wilt-using-solar-powered-kinetic-art-to-communicate-climate-change/)
