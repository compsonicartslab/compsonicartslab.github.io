### Summary

This is the second meeting of the academic year 2026-2027. The main topic of the meeting is: Lab's mission revisited. We will also discuss aesthetics in our practices (NIME, creative AI, sonic arts, sound and music computing...)

### Agenda

* Round of updates
* Revision of the lab's mission text
* Discussion of aesthetics in your practice
* Other, ideas for future meetings
* Closing
