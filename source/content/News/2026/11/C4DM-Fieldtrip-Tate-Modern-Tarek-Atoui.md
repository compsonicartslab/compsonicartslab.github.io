**Day/time**: 3/11/2026 11am-12.30pm

**Location**: Turbine Hall at Tate Modern

Charalampos Saitis (COMMA lab) and I (CSAL lab), who are module facilitators of the module Interactive Digital Multimedia Techniques, decided to organise a field trip to visit [Tarek Atoui's sound installation at Turbine Hall (Tate Modern)](https://www.tate.org.uk/whats-on/tate-modern/tarek-atoui) with the IDMT students, an invitation that we extended to our respective labs and C4DM.

The field trip was amazing!

You can read more about the visit in detail in this blog post, written by Nico, one of our lab members:

* [C4DM Field Trip: Tarek Atoui at Turbine Hall by Nico García-Peguinho](https://c4dm.eecs.qmul.ac.uk/news/2026-11-04.C4DM-fieldtrip_tate/)

The visit has also been highlighted in the EECS news, thanks to Aurélie Leroy:

* [C4DM students and researchers explore sound, technology and interaction at Tate Modern](https://www.qmul.ac.uk/eecs/news-and-events/news/items/c4dm-students-and-researchers-explore-sound-technology-and-interaction-at-tate-modern.html)

Thanks, everyone, for a great morning on the field trip!
